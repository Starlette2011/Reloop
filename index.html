Reloop

Local recycling rules for supported cities, bin audits that measure contamination, and surplus food routing. Runs entirely in your browser.

Sort an item

Type your city and an item. Supported cities give cited local answers. Other places get clearly labeled general guidance. Unknown or unclear items get the safe option.

Audit a bin

List items found in a bin or load. Reloop reports the contamination rate: the share of items that do not belong in recycling.

Route surplus food

Prototype with sample food banks. Matches a listing to the nearest one that accepts that category and tracks pounds diverted.
