Reloop

Local recycling rules for supported cities, bin audits, surplus food routing, and a city view of which mistakes to fix next. Runs entirely in your browser.

Sort an item

Type your city and an item. Supported cities give cited local answers. Other places get clearly labeled general guidance. Unknown items get the safe option.

Audit a bin

List items found in a bin or load. Reloop reports the contamination rate: the share of items that do not belong in recycling.

Route surplus food

Prototype with sample food banks. Matches a listing to the nearest one that accepts that category and tracks pounds diverted.

City view

The most common items sent to non-recycling bins, from the scan log on this device, with ready-to-print bin-tag messages.
