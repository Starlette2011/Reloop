Reloop

Local recycling rules for supported cities, bin audits that measure contamination, and surplus food routing. Photo analysis runs on your device.

Sort an item

Type your city, then take a photo or type an item. Photos are analyzed on your device and never uploaded. Unclear photos get the safe option.

Audit a bin

Add photos of items from a bin or load, or list them. Reloop reports the contamination rate: the share of items that do not belong in recycling. You can download the results as a CSV.

Route surplus food

Prototype with sample food banks. Matches a listing to the nearest one that accepts that category and tracks pounds diverted.
