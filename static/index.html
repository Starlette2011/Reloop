Reloop

Local recycling rules for households, bin audits for operators, surplus food routing, and a city view of which mistakes to fix next. One scan log feeds all four.

Sort an item

Photo or name. The answer follows your city's rules and cites them. Low confidence returns the safe option.

Audit a bin

Upload photos of items pulled from a bin or load. Reloop reports the contamination rate: the share of items that do not belong in recycling.

Route surplus food

List what a store or restaurant can't sell. Reloop matches it to the nearest food bank that accepts that category and tracks pounds diverted.

City view

The most common items sent to non-recycling bins, from the shared scan log, with ready-to-print bin-tag messages.
